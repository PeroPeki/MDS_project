mission 1

What is gamification?

“The use of game design elements in non-game contexts”

Deterding et al. (2011)

Gamification does not mean playing video games at work. It means using elements like points, achievements and progress bars in everyday activities to increase engagement.

Points

Instant feedback for completing activities and learning challenges.

Badges

A visible sign of something achieved, such as finishing a mission.

Leaderboards

Rankings can motivate but may encourage unhealthy competition.

mission 4

When gamification backfires

Rewards can help motivation, but badly designed incentives may hurt team collaboration.

Mixed results

Research finds that gamification effects vary by context and user. Source: Hamari et al. (2014).

Competition over collaboration

Individual leaderboards can pit teammates against each other instead of rewarding shared outcomes.

Gaming the metric

If points reward ticket counts rather than value, teams may optimize the score instead of real outcomes.
